## Full description
MZDATA is a premium VTU and payments app built for fast mobile transactions.

Fund your wallet through your dedicated virtual account, buy data, top up airtime, pay bills, and track every transaction with clean receipts and history.

Designed for clarity, trust, and speed, MZDATA gives you a calm transaction experience with secure approvals and a polished account center.

### Key features
- Dedicated virtual account wallet funding
- Buy data, airtime, electricity, and more
- Transaction PIN protection for sensitive actions
- Premium receipts and shareable proof of payment
- Transaction history and details
- In-app notifications for important account activity
- Light and dark mode support
